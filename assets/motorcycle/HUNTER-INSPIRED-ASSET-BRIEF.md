# Hunter-Inspired Motorcycle Asset Brief

Create an ORIGINAL modern-classic roadster.

Inspiration:
- compact urban roadster
- round headlamp
- compact tank
- exposed engine
- upright rider triangle
- short exhaust
- premium simple silhouette

Do NOT copy:
- Royal Enfield logo
- badges
- exact model graphics
- proprietary renders
- exact commercial livery

Goal:
A believable original roadster that communicates the same broad category and mood without pretending to be an official motorcycle model.
