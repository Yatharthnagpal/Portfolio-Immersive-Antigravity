Avoid: cyberpunk overload, neon rainbow, futuristic sports bike, sci-fi motorcycle, fake logos, brand logos, toy motorcycle, cartoon anatomy, generic AI robot, random text, watermarks, excessive glow, low-quality face, distorted face, extra limbs, malformed hands.
