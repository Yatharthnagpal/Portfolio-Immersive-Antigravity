# Design Rules

## Core aesthetic
Premium modern-classic roadster × cinematic automotive film × AI systems HUD × dark editorial design.

## Palette
- Background: #050706 / #080B09 / #0D110E
- Deep forest green
- Warm metallic green
- Soft lime highlight
- Warm white typography
- Graphite / brushed metal surfaces

Green is an accent, not a neon wash.

## Motorcycle
Original design inspired by modern-classic roadster proportions:
- round headlamp
- compact tank
- exposed engine
- upright stance
- short exhaust
- roadster wheels
- compact tail
- premium metallic materials

Do not reproduce Royal Enfield logos, badges, trademarks, or proprietary graphics.

## Typography
Display: modern geometric sans-serif.
Body: clean sans-serif.
HUD: monospace.

## Avoid
- generic cyberpunk
- excessive neon
- random floating shapes
- constant spinning
- toy-like motorcycle geometry
- sports-bike fairings
- visual noise without narrative purpose
- ordinary card grids as the primary project presentation
